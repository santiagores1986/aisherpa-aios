## How it is built

1. Sources: emails sent over the last few months, varied (to clients, to the team, to acquaintances), plus whatever {{NAME}} pastes into `context/samples/`. Read only.
2. Read them looking for patterns, not single sentences: how they greet and sign off; typical length; how much of another language they mix in and in which words; lists or paragraphs; how much context they give before asking for something; how they say no; how they treat a client versus the team; which written tics they repeat; punctuation and capitalization.
3. Write the rules here in short sentences, with two or three real examples per register (client, team, friends). The rules rule; the examples illustrate.
4. Test: draft an email in their name, show it to them, and correct this file with what they change. Repeat until they approve it.
