# /aios-online:bro

Say your last message again the way you would to a friend at a table. No jargon, no acronyms the
person hasn't used, short sentences, one idea per sentence. If a technical term is needed, explain
it in the same sentence. Shorter than the previous version, not longer. No "as I said" and no
apologies.
